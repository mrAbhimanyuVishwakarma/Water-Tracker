Engineered for Mindful Hydration

Discover how Brownie Water Tracker transforms daily fluid intake into a calming, rewarding habit grounded in real mathematics and user respect.

🧮

Personalized Hydration Formula

Instead of forcing a generic 2-liter rule on everyone, Brownie calculates a personalized target between 1,500 ml and 4,500 ml using your physical profile:

  • Base Requirement: 35 ml per kilogram of body weight (supporting both kilograms and pounds).
  • Activity Adjustment: Adds +350 ml for moderate activity, +700 ml for high activity, and +1,100 ml for athletes.
  • Climate Adjustment: Adds +400 ml for hot environments and +300 ml for dry climates.
  • Custom Flexibility: Override the recommendation anytime with your own target in milliliters (ml) or fluid ounces (oz).

Truthful Streaks & Monthly Calendar

Many habit apps manufacture fake achievements to keep you clicking. Brownie keeps it honest:

  • Saved Data Only: A day counts as completed only when your logged hydration reaches your configured goal.
  • Visual Calendar: A dedicated monthly calendar displays every successful goal day with a celebratory droplet dot.
  • Habit Prompts: Receive mindful prompts (like drinking a glass upon waking or rehydrating post-workout) without pressure.
🌙

Smart Sleep-Aware Reminders

Brownie respects your rest. Notifications are scheduled locally strictly within your configured waking schedule:

  • Custom Wake & Sleep Times: Set your active hours (for example, wake at 07:30 and sleep at 23:00).
  • Zero Middle-of-the-Night Alerts: Reminders cancel and reschedule automatically outside waking windows.
  • Dedicated Android Notification Channel: Switch between subtle quiet chime or standard sound channel directly in Android settings.
