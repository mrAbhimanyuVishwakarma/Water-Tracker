01 / Your own pace

A goal with you in mind.

Set a target that suits your routine, or let Brownie suggest one based on your profile.

Switch between millilitres and US fluid ounces, customize your cups, and adjust your goal whenever you need.

How the suggestion works

Starting point
35 ml per kg of body weight
Activity
+350 to +1,100 ml
Climate
+300 ml dry / +400 ml hot
Suggested range
1,500–4,500 ml per day

An app estimate for habit tracking. Individual needs vary; follow your clinician’s advice if you have a prescribed fluid target.

Your cup of tea counts, too.

03 / A timely nudge

A reminder. Not an interruption.

Choose your wake and sleep times. Brownie schedules reminders around your waking hours, so your rest stays yours.

Adjust or disable notifications in settings. For help with Android battery restrictions, visit our reminder guide.

04 / Small wins

Progress you can look back on.

Daily drink history and a monthly calendar keep your routine in view. A completed goal day counts toward your streak.

Your history reflects the drinks you’ve logged, giving you a simple picture of how your habit is taking shape.
