A few things you might be wondering

A little clarity.

Everything else? We’re happy to help in the support center.

Is Brownie free to use?

Yes. Drink logging, daily goals, reminders, history, and Brownie the capybara are free. An optional Pro upgrade unlocks five more animal companions.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest. Your drinks and preferences are stored on your phone. Sign in only if you want optional cloud backup.

Can I log drinks other than water?

Yes. Choose from 11 beverages, including tea, coffee, juice, milk, and electrolytes. The app applies its own hydration estimates to each drink. These estimates support habit tracking and are not medical advice.

Will reminders wake me up?

Set your wake and sleep times in the app. Reminders are scheduled within your chosen waking hours, and you can turn them off anytime.

Where can I download the app?

The Android beta is available through our official GitHub Releases. Google Play is in preparation; an iOS download is not currently available.

How do I delete my data?

For local data, use Reset All Data in the app’s settings. For cloud accounts, use Settings → Account → Delete Account. See the account deletion page for more details.
