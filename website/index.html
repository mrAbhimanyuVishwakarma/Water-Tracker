Privacy First

Your Wellness Data Stays Under Your Control

Brownie Water Tracker is designed with strong privacy principles. We don't sell personal data, we don't serve third-party ads, and we make account deletion completely transparent.

Read Privacy Policy Account Deletion Rights
  • ✓ Guest Mode by Default: All hydration logs are saved locally on your phone using AsyncStorage without creating any remote account.
  • ✓ Optional Cloud Backup: Synchronize across devices with Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring only you access your data.
  • ✓ Google Play Billing via RevenueCat: Payments are processed directly by Google Play. We never handle or store your payment cards.
  • ✓ Complete Account Deletion: Instantly wipe your remote profile and hydration records in-app or via our public deletion page.
