Google Play & Privacy Compliance

Account & Data Deletion

Transparent instructions to delete your cloud account and associated hydration records.

App Name: Brownie Water Tracker  |  Package ID: com.brownie.watertracker  |  Backend: Supabase Auth & PostgreSQL RLS

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies and international privacy laws, this page provides clear instructions on how you can request the permanent deletion of your Brownie Water Tracker account and associated data.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the app installed on your Android device and have a connected cloud account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data in seconds:

  1. Open Brownie Water Tracker on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings button in the top header.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Cloud Backup section and tap MANAGE.
  4. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the account modal.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and confirm by tapping Delete My Account.

What happens automatically: The app invokes our authenticated Supabase Edge Function (delete-account), which deletes your user identity from Supabase Auth and automatically triggers a PostgreSQL cascade delete removing your profile record in hydration_profiles and every logged drink in hydration_logs. Your local account credentials and tokens are removed immediately.

Option 2: External Deletion Request (Without Reinstalling)

If you no longer have the app installed, lost your Android device, or prefer to request deletion externally, you do not need to reinstall the app. You may submit an external deletion request through either of the following channels:

  • GitHub Issues: Open a private or confidential deletion request on our verified repository tracker: https://github.com/mrAbhimanyuVishwakarma/Water-Tracker/issues
  • Direct Email: Email watertrackerbrownie.vercel.app/support with the subject line “Account Deletion Request - Brownie Water Tracker”.

Identity Verification & Turnaround Time

To protect user privacy and prevent unauthorized account deletions, you must provide the exact Google OAuth email address or mobile phone number associated with your account. A verification email or SMS confirmation code will be sent to confirm ownership before deletion is executed.

Verified external deletion requests are processed within 3 to 7 business days. Once completed, a confirmation notice will be sent.

What Cloud Data Is Permanently Deleted

Data Category Data Elements Action Taken
Account Identity User ID UUID, email address, phone number, auth tokens Permanently deleted from Supabase Auth
Hydration Profile Goal ml, unit preferences, wake/sleep hours, weight settings Permanently wiped via foreign key cascade
Hydration History All recorded drink logs, timestamps, beverage types Permanently erased from database

Guest Mode & Local Device Data

If you use Brownie Water Tracker as a guest without cloud backup, your data exists solely on your phone in local storage. You can delete all local guest data at any time by:

  • Using the in-app Reset All Data button in Settings; or
  • Opening Android Settings → Apps → Brownie Water Tracker → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data; or
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

Important: Google Play Subscriptions Must Be Cancelled Separately

⚠️ CRITICAL SUBSCRIPTION WARNING: Deleting your Brownie Water Tracker account or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscriptions are billed and managed directly by Google Play.

To avoid unwanted renewal charges, cancel your subscription through Google Play before or immediately after deleting your account:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. Select Brownie Water Tracker and tap Cancel subscription.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm cancellation.

Data Retained by Google or Third Parties

Google maintains purchase records and transaction invoices in accordance with financial regulations and tax compliance laws. The developer of Brownie Water Tracker does not hold, access, or have the ability to delete Google's internal financial records.
