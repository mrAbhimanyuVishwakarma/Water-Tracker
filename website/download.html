A quick install guide

From download
to your first sip.

  1. Download the APK.

    Open this page on your Android phone and download the latest APK from our official GitHub release.

  2. Open the downloaded file.

    Your browser or Android may ask you to confirm the download. Check that the file comes from this project’s official release.

  3. Allow your browser to install.

    If prompted, open Settings and enable “Allow from this source” for the browser you used. You can turn this off afterward.

  4. Meet your new companion.

    Tap Install, open Brownie, and choose your daily hydration goal.

Only install files from this website or our official GitHub repository. Need a hand? Visit support.
