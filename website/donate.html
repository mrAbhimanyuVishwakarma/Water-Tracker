How to view Pro plans

  1. Open Brownie on Android and go to Settings → Brownie Premium.
  2. Sign in to view available plans. Current prices and purchase terms are shown in the app and by Google Play.
  3. Already purchased Pro? Choose Restore Purchases using the same Google Play account.

Pro purchases will be available in the Google Play release. You can try the free Android beta now.
