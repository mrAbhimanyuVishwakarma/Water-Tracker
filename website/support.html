Reminders Not Delivering on Android

Android aggressive battery optimization can sometimes delay local alarms. To fix this:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Brownie Water Tracker.
  • Tap Battery and select Unrestricted.
  • Confirm notifications are enabled in App Settings → Notifications.
  • Verify your Wake / Sleep hours in App Settings to ensure reminders are scheduled during the active window.

Cloud Backup & Offline Sync

Brownie works fully offline. Drinks logged without internet are safely stored on your device:

  • Pending drinks upload automatically upon reconnecting to the internet.
  • In the event of a sync conflict across multiple devices, your unsynced local logs take priority to prevent data loss.
  • Check backup status in Settings → Account & Cloud Backup.

Restoring Purchases Across Devices

If you unlocked Pro mascots on another phone or reinstalled:

  • Ensure you are logged into the same Google Play account that made the purchase.
  • Open Brownie Settings → Tap the Pro Banner → Tap Restore Purchases.
  • RevenueCat verifies your active entitlement and instantly restores Pro mascots.

Do I need to create an account? +
No. Guest mode is enabled by default. All your hydration logs and settings remain stored on your device using AsyncStorage. Cloud account sign-in (via Google OAuth or SMS OTP) is completely optional if you wish to back up your data across devices.
Why does coffee or tea have a different hydration multiplier? +
Beverages with caffeine or sugar have different net hydration impacts. For instance, coffee has an approximate 0.80x multiplier to account for mild diuretic effects, while green and herbal teas have a 0.95x multiplier. Pure water is 1.0x, and alcoholic drinks carry a negative multiplier (-0.50x) because alcohol actively dehydrates your body.
Can I switch between Milliliters (ml) and Fluid Ounces (oz)? +
Yes! In the app's Settings screen, tap the unit toggle to seamlessly switch between milliliters (ml) and US fluid ounces (oz). All progress rings, logs, and cup sizes automatically convert.
How do I delete my account or reset my data? +
You can delete your cloud account directly in Settings → Account → Delete Account. If you are using Guest mode, you can tap "Reset All Data" in Settings or clear app storage in Android system settings. If you cannot access the app, visit our Account Deletion Page to request deletion externally.
