Missing a reminder?

Android battery settings can delay notifications. Here are a few things to check:

  1. Allow notifications for Brownie in your phone’s app settings.
  2. Check your wake and sleep times inside Brownie.
  3. If reminders are still delayed, check battery optimization settings for the app.

The exact settings vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

Checking your backup.

Your guest logs live on your phone. If you use an account, check your connection and cloud backup status in the app’s settings.

Before resetting or reinstalling, make sure any data you want to keep has been backed up.

Restoring your friends.

If you’ve purchased Pro, use the same Google Play account as your original purchase.

Open the Pro screen in Brownie’s settings and choose Restore Purchases. If you still need help, open a support issue.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Here are a few things to know about life with Brownie.

Is Brownie free to use?

Yes. Drink logging, daily goals, reminders, history, and Brownie the capybara are free. An optional Pro upgrade unlocks five more animal companions.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest. Your drinks and preferences are stored on your phone. Sign in only if you want optional cloud backup.

Can I log drinks other than water?

Yes. Choose from 11 beverages, including tea, coffee, juice, milk, and electrolytes. The app applies its own hydration estimates to each drink. These estimates support habit tracking and are not medical advice.

Will reminders wake me up?

Set your wake and sleep times in the app. Reminders are scheduled within your chosen waking hours, and you can turn them off anytime.

Where can I download the app?

The Android beta is available through our official GitHub Releases. Google Play is in preparation; an iOS download is not currently available.

How do I delete my data?

For local data, use Reset All Data in the app’s settings. For cloud accounts, use Settings → Account → Delete Account. See the account deletion page for more details.

Can I switch to fluid ounces?

Yes. Change the unit preference in the app’s settings to switch between millilitres and US fluid ounces.
