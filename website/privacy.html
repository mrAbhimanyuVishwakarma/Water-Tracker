Privacy Policy

How Brownie Water Tracker handles local storage, optional cloud backup, notifications, and user data rights.

Effective Date: October 28, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 28, 2026  |  App Package: com.brownie.watertracker

Thank you for using Brownie Water Tracker (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains our collection, use, and protection of information when you use our Android mobile application and related website (watertrackerbrownie.vercel.app).

1. Core Principles: Local-First & Privacy-Focused

We believe personal health habits should remain personal. The App operates primarily on a local-first model:

  • Guest Usage: You can use all core features (goal calculation, beverage logging, streaks, reminder setup, and Brownie's mascot party) without creating an account or providing any personal identifying information.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The App does not contain third-party ad banners, advertising SDKs, or data broker trackers.
  • No Medical Data Selling: We never sell, monetize, or rent your hydration logs or physical parameters to advertisers, insurers, or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect and Store

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use the App as a guest, all information is stored exclusively on your device using React Native AsyncStorage:

  • Physical & Hydration Parameters: Gender, weight, weight unit (kg or lbs), activity level, climate setting, waking time, and sleeping time. These are used locally to calculate your suggested daily hydration goal.
  • Hydration Drink Logs: Beverage type (e.g., water, green tea, coffee), volume amount in ml or oz, effective hydration amount, drink color, and local timestamp.
  • Preferences: Sound effects, haptic feedback toggles, and customized drink cup presets.

B. Information Collected When You Choose Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you voluntarily choose to sign in to back up your hydration history across devices, authentication and cloud data synchronization are handled via Supabase:

  • Authentication Identifiers: Your Google OAuth account email/name or mobile phone number (via SMS OTP). Authentication uses browser OAuth with PKCE. Passwords are never collected or stored by the App.
  • Cloud Hydration Records: Your synced hydration profile (settings) and hydration log records (hydration_profiles and hydration_logs tables).
  • Database Security: PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies enforce that your cloud data can only be accessed, inserted, or updated by your authenticated user session.

C. In-App Purchases and Billing Information

Optional premium mascot upgrades and subscriptions are processed by Google Play:

  • Payment transactions are conducted securely through Google Play In-App Billing.
  • Entitlement verification is tracked via RevenueCat using your anonymous Supabase User ID.
  • We never see, collect, or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details. All payment processing is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions Used

The Android application requests only the minimum native permissions necessary to function:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for optional Supabase cloud synchronization, Google OAuth sign-in, and RevenueCat purchase entitlement checks.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to deliver your configured daily hydration reminders during your chosen waking hours.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used for pleasant, subtle tactile haptic feedback when logging a drink (can be disabled in Settings).

Permissions We Explicitly Block and Do Not Request: The app explicitly removes and never requests camera, microphone, external device storage, exact location, or install-packages permissions. Android OS system backup (allowBackup=false) is disabled to prevent cloud extraction of local data outside your explicit Supabase account.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Stored locally until you choose to reset your data in Settings, clear App storage in Android system settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud Data: Retained in Supabase until you delete your account.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time. In-app deletion (Settings → Account → Delete Account) triggers an automated Supabase Edge Function that purges your account from Auth and cascades to permanently delete your hydration_profiles and hydration_logs. For external requests, visit our Account Deletion Page.
  • Note on Subscriptions: Deleting your app account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled through your Google Play Store account.

5. Children’s Privacy

Brownie Water Tracker is a general audience wellness application designed for adults and young people learning healthy habits. We do not knowingly collect personal identifying information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions) without parental consent. If we learn that personal data of a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that data.

6. International Data Processing

Cloud data (if you enable account sign-in) is hosted securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure in data centers subject to industry standard encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/HTTPS).

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (such as under the EU GDPR, UK GDPR, or California CCPA), you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Review your stored hydration logs and account information.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate weight or hydration parameters.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Permanently delete your account and hydration history.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain an export of your saved drink records.

8. Website Analytics and Cookies

When visiting our website (watertrackerbrownie.vercel.app), no third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies are loaded by default. Optional anonymous usage analytics are loaded only after your explicit consent via our consent banner. You can change your choice anytime via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, legal requirements, or store policies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or data privacy requests regarding Brownie Water Tracker, please open a public request on our verified GitHub Issues page or contact our developer:

  • Developer: mrAbhimanyuVishwakarma
