BROWNIE WATER TRACKER - PRODUCT IDEAS
Last updated: 13 November 2026

PURPOSE
This file records future product ideas only. Implement features one at a time after the interaction, privacy model, platform behavior, and reference designs are agreed.


1. USER PROFILE AND HEALTH PROFILE

Status: Basic editable profile form implemented in v0.0.2 (name, gender, weight, height, health context, medication notes, and appointment notes). Profile photo, date of birth, allergies, emergency contacts, dedicated export/delete controls, and field-level cloud encryption remain future work.

Add a User Profile section inside Settings, near Account & Cloud Backup.

Possible profile information:
- Name, profile photo, date of birth, and gender (optional)
- Height and weight, including metric and imperial units
- Activity level, climate, wake time, and sleep time
- Hydration and wellness goals
- Medical conditions or diseases (optional and user-entered)
- Allergies (optional)
- Whether the user takes regular medicines
- Emergency contact information (optional)

Product requirements:
- Every sensitive field must be optional.
- Explain why each field is requested and how it changes the experience.
- Allow users to view, edit, export, and permanently delete their health data.
- Encrypt sensitive cloud data and avoid placing medical details in analytics or logs.
- Clearly state that the app does not diagnose conditions or replace medical advice.
- Health conditions and medicines must not automatically change hydration targets without a carefully reviewed safety model.


2. MEDICINE TRACKING

Status: The v0.0.2 profile can store medication notes only. Scheduling, dose records, reminders, and adherence tracking remain future work.

Allow users to record and track regular medicines.

Possible capabilities:
- Medicine name, dosage, form, instructions, and optional notes
- Schedule by time, day, interval, or as-needed status
- Start date, end date, and refill reminder
- Taken, skipped, missed, or snoozed status
- Medication history and adherence summary
- Optional link between a medicine and a medical condition
- Reminder notification with safe actions such as Taken, Snooze, or Open App

Safety requirements:
- Never recommend a dosage or tell users to start, stop, or combine medicines.
- Do not infer medical advice from adherence data.
- Use neutral reminder language and include a clear emergency disclaimer where relevant.
- Require deliberate confirmation before recording a dose from a notification.


3. MEDICAL APPOINTMENTS AND CALENDAR

Status: The v0.0.2 profile can store appointment notes only. Structured appointments and calendar connection remain future work.

Add appointment tracking for doctor visits, tests, therapy, pharmacy visits, and other fixed medical events.

Possible appointment information:
- Appointment title and type
- Doctor or clinic name
- Date, time, timezone, duration, and location
- Preparation notes, documents, questions, and follow-up tasks
- Reminder timing
- Completed, cancelled, or rescheduled status

Google Calendar plan:
- Offer Google Calendar connection only after Google sign-in and explicit permission.
- Let users choose which appointments are added to the calendar.
- Support creating, updating, and removing the linked calendar event.
- Store the calendar event ID so app and calendar changes can be reconciled.
- Request the smallest possible Google Calendar permission scope.
- Clearly explain whether synchronization is one-way or two-way before enabling it.

Future consideration:
- Apple Calendar and device-calendar support should be evaluated separately for iOS.


4. ACCOUNT AND CLOUD BACKUP EXPANSION

Extend the existing Account & Cloud Backup area to include profile, health, medicine, and appointment data.

Possible controls:
- Last successful backup time
- Manual Sync Now action
- Per-category sync controls
- Conflict handling when two devices edit the same record
- Export all user data
- Delete cloud backup
- Delete account and all associated health data
- Clear explanation of what remains only on-device and what is stored in the cloud


5. NOTIFICATION QUICK-LOG PANEL

Status: Idea only. Wait for the user's screenshots and interaction references before designing or implementing.

Hydration reminder concept:
- A reminder appears as an interactive notification surface.
- Users can quickly log their normal water amount without opening the app.
- Additional actions may include choosing another amount, snoozing, or opening the app.
- The app must prevent accidental duplicate logs and show clear confirmation.

iOS direction:
- Study Dynamic Island and Live Activities behavior after references are supplied.
- Respect Apple's interaction, timing, privacy, and Live Activity limitations.
- Do not assume every iPhone supports Dynamic Island.

Android direction:
- Design an equivalent experience using notification actions and supported live-notification patterns.
- Keep feature behavior consistent even when the visual presentation differs from iOS.

Open decisions for the future:
- Default amount and available custom amounts
- Undo behavior
- Whether the notification remains visible after logging
- Lock-screen privacy
- Animation timing and visual style
- Behavior when the phone is offline


6. LIVING, INTERACTIVE CHARACTERS

Goal:
Make each mascot feel alive and responsive, inspired by virtual-pet interactions such as Talking Tom, while keeping every character original to Brownie Water Tracker.

Interaction ideas:
- Tap ears: character-specific ear reaction
- Tap face or head: facial expression or playful response
- Tap belly, hands, feet, shell, trunk, wings, or another character-specific body area
- Double tap: a distinct surprise or celebration animation
- Long press: another optional reaction
- Swipe direction: look, lean, wave, spin, or dodge
- Idle reactions after a period without interaction
- Hydration-state reactions for empty, partially filled, almost complete, and goal completed
- Time-of-day reactions for morning, afternoon, evening, and sleep hours
- Streak and milestone celebrations

Implementation principles:
- Each mascot needs its own interaction map because body regions differ.
- Define tappable regions with invisible hit areas that scale with the character artwork.
- Prevent competing gestures from triggering multiple animations at once.
- Use a small state machine: idle, reacting, drinking, celebrating, and resting.
- Queue or interrupt animations intentionally.
- Respect Reduce Motion and provide a calm static alternative.
- Pause idle animation when the screen or app is not visible.
- Keep animation assets optimized for mobile memory, battery, and download size.
- Avoid sound on every interaction; respect the existing sound and haptic settings.
- Preserve accessibility labels and alternative actions for users who cannot tap precise body regions.

Asset plan for every mascot:
- Neutral idle loop
- Blink and look-around variations
- Ear or head reaction
- Face reaction
- Character-specific body reaction
- Double-tap surprise
- Drinking animation
- Goal-completion celebration
- Resting first frame for each animation


SUGGESTED IMPLEMENTATION ORDER

Phase 1 - Product and privacy foundation
1. Define the health-profile fields and consent language.
2. Define local and cloud data models, encryption, export, and deletion behavior.
3. Add the basic editable User Profile and Health Profile screens.

Phase 2 - Medicine and appointments
4. Build medicine records and in-app schedules without notification actions first.
5. Build appointment records and an in-app agenda.
6. Add carefully scoped reminders.
7. Add Google Calendar connection after sign-in and permission UX are approved.

Phase 3 - Notification quick actions
8. Review the user's Dynamic Island and notification references.
9. Specify iOS and Android equivalents.
10. Prototype quick log, confirmation, undo, offline behavior, and duplicate prevention.

Phase 4 - Interactive mascots
11. Prototype the full interaction state machine with Brownie.
12. Validate gesture conflicts, performance, accessibility, and Reduce Motion.
13. Produce character-specific assets and interaction maps for the remaining mascots.
