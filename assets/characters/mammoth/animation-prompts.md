# Manni: individual mammoth reaction video prompts

This pack belongs to the existing `mammoth/` character folder. Every prompt below is self-contained: attach the shared master image and copy ONE complete block. Generate one original MP4 per action.

## Shared master frame

Use `assets/characters/mammoth/sources/reaction-master.png` as both the first and last frame of every clip. It preserves the existing `poster.png` pixel-for-pixel in the central 720-by-720 area of a 1280-by-720 white canvas; Manni's artwork has not been redesigned. Keep that same canvas and framing for the whole set.

Where the generator exposes first/end-frame controls, put the SAME image in both slots. [Google Flow supports start and end frames](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353334?hl=en). [Gemini Apps accepts image/video references and audio instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16126339?hl=en). Available controls depend on the selected model and interface. Prompts alone cannot guarantee pixel-identical endpoints; check the downloads for drift.

Use `assets/characters/mammoth/sipping.mp4` as an optional reference for Manni's anatomy and existing drinking motion. Keep one chosen Manni voice configuration consistent across the batch. Do not import background music or an unsuitable loud voice from a reference.

## Manni's anatomy and performance

His large ears, rigid ivory tusks, flexible trunk, blue knitted scarf, feet and tufted tail each have their own motion constraints. The trunk starts INSIDE the glass; the master contains NO straw. Every clip must return the trunk tip to that same resting position without passing through a glass wall. Directions always mean the viewer's left/right, not anatomical left/right.

Loop-ready means: shared still → one natural reaction → exactly the same shared still. The app will play a touch reaction once, then hold the still. Do not fill a generation with repeated actions or reverse the movement to manufacture an ending.

Keep the full natural duration of every vocalization and movement, including longer reactions when they need time. There is no one- or two-second cap. Leave unused clip time quietly in the final rest pose instead of rushing, stretching or repeating a sound.

Generate **trunk**, **tail**, **face/eye** and **happy bounce** first to settle his motion and voice before completing the rest. Eye touch should give a single “Oh!”, tail a single two-word “No, no.” phrase, and double tap a genuine giggle rather than robotic spoken cheering. Tusks should never flex or detach.

The four directional gaze clips are optional for fixed-direction reactions. A prerecorded video cannot continuously track an arbitrary moving finger; smooth live following still requires the app's pupil code. Keep directional gaze clips silent to avoid repeated sounds as the finger changes direction.

## Files to return

Save the original MP4 downloads, with their embedded audio intact, under `assets/characters/mammoth/sources/reactions/`, using the filenames below. Keep the shared master unchanged throughout the batch.

Check: one intended action and sound; complete body visible; consistent voice; rigid tusks; continuous trunk; stable glass; exact return to the same first-frame pose, scale, gaze, grip, trunk depth and water level. On integration, a clip's own audio should play once without layering a separate reaction WAV over it. This pack prepares assets; Manni-specific app gestures and touch maps remain implementation work after the videos are ready.
